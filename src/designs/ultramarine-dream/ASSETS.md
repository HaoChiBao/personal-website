# Asset Direction (Ultramarine Dream)

## Core Style Language
The visual system should feel like:
- a dream in ultramarine blue
- a blurred silhouette emerging through blue mist
- soft pigment floating through white space
- minimal surreal editorial design
- atmospheric fine-art technology branding

Global constraints:
- Monochrome ultramarine only (primary/cobalt/icy glow).
- Avoid warm tones and overly saturated multicolor palettes.
- Avoid “glossy 3D” and neon cyberpunk aesthetics.
- Use grain/noise and soft diffusion blur at the edges of shapes.

## Texture & Color Treatments
- Grain: subtle airbrush-like noise overlay on hero sections, surfaces, and large blocks.
- Fog gradients: layered radial gradients with low contrast transitions.
- Glowing edges: soft bloom (use glow, not hard strokes).
- Composition: minimal, spacious, strong negative space.

## Icon Direction
Style:
- Simplified geometry, elegant silhouettes, minimal detail.
- Monochrome treatment: single-color ultramarine with soft glow or slight opacity.
- Edges should look diffused (slight blur/bloom), not crisp.

Production prompts:
- “minimal ultramarine line icon with soft diffusion blur edges, grain overlay, calm cinematic lighting”

Size guidance:
- Primary icons: 20-28px within the UI.
- Ensure icons remain legible with reduced opacity and at small sizes.

## Illustration & Abstract Graphics
Style:
- Abstract silhouette-like forms and foggy gradients.
- Blur and depth: background elements should never be sharply defined.
- Keep the composition editorial: fewer elements, more whitespace.

Prompt examples:
- “blurred memory silhouette emerging from blue mist, monochrome ultramarine, grainy airbrush texture, fog gradient background, minimal abstract composition”
- “abstract cloud-pigment forms, monochrome cobalt-ultramarine, soft diffusion, negative space”

## Hero Graphics (Website Banners)
Visual requirements:
- One hero motif per section: a silhouette-like shape or a fog bloom.
- Background should be a layered fog gradient (not a hard image cutout).
- If using photography/portraits: monochrome/near-monochrome blue tint, soft focus, vignette, and diffusion edges.

## Social Media Assets
Templates:
- Square (1080x1080), portrait (1080x1350), and story (1080x1920) variations.
- Background: near-white cool fog with ultramarine radial blooms.
- Typography: short headline + minimal supporting text.

## Loading Screens & Motion Assets (Visual Only)
- Circular progress / airy ring shapes with ultramarine gradient (no rainbow).
- Subtle bloom and grain; calm fog gradient background.

## Production Checklist
Before shipping any asset:
- Is it monochrome ultramarine (primary/cobalt/icy glow)?
- Are edges softly diffused (no hard cutouts)?
- Is there subtle grain/noise on surfaces?
- Does it remain readable against both light and dark theme backgrounds?
- Does the design respect reduced motion (no spinning/looping implied visuals)?
