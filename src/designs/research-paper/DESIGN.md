# Research Paper

## Status
`ready`

## Visual identity
Personal site framed as a short academic article: running header, centered title/author block, abstract, numbered sections, figure captions, and a references list. Cool paper white + charcoal ink — not a newspaper broadsheet.

## Goals
- Read as one manuscript composition
- Projects appear as “results” with figures
- Contact + résumé live in correspondence / bibliography

## Color
- Paper: `#F7F7F5`
- Ink: `#1A1A1A`
- Muted: `#5C5C5C`
- Rule: `rgba(26,26,26,0.18)`
- Accent (links): `#1A3A5C` (ink blue, not purple)

## Type
- Display / body: `Tiempos Fine`
- Meta / captions: `Aeonik`
