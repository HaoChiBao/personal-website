# Split Rail

## Status
`ready`

## Visual identity
Two-column résumé site in the spirit of nathanwan.ca: sticky left rail (name, bio, work, links), scrolling right column of project sections. Pure typography — no cards, borders, or shadows. Quiet greys, gold star accent on section hover.

## Goals
- Read as one clean composition, not a dashboard
- Hover reveals dates (work) and dims sibling projects
- Arc underline on a bio keyword; spinning ★ on section hover

## Color
- Light: bg `#fff`, text `#1d1d1f`, org `#3a3a3c`, muted `#515154` / `#6e6e73`, star `#d99e00`
- Dark (system): bg `#191919`, text `#ededef`, adjusted greys, star `#ffd60a`

## Type
Aeonik (site font) with system sans fallbacks. Name 21px/600, body 17px, eyebrows 12px uppercase tracked.
